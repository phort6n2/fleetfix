Safe drive-away time

A bonded windshield is a structural component of your vehicle. It contributes to roof strength in a rollover and it backs the passenger airbag. The urethane adhesive must reach a minimum strength before the vehicle is safe to drive, and how long that takes depends on the adhesive, the temperature and the humidity.

Your technician will give you a specific safe drive-away time and you must observe it. If you drive the vehicle before that time, you do so against our express instruction, and we accept no responsibility for the consequences, including the glass moving, the bond failing, or the windshield not performing as intended in a collision.
